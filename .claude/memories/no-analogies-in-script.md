# No analogies in script

The script of every production made in this repo must not use analogies, even where the audience is the general public. It is the user's rule, and it holds for every production (see [The repo is a media factory](the-repo-is-a-media-factory.md)).

**Why:** No reason comes with the rule. Claude's interpretation: the user wants the real mechanism of a subject explained in plain language, not replaced by a comparison to something else.

**How to apply:** In the narration script and on-screen text of any production, describe what the subject actually does and define terms plainly. For the Gödel machines film, whose audience is the general public, assume no maths or programming background. Do not write comparisons or metaphors that stand in for the mechanism. The pictures are different: the user allows them to use metaphor the way Kurzgesagt's do. So the narration stays literal, and the pictures may draw ideas as objects, creatures, or places, as long as they do not contradict the real mechanism the voice describes. Related: [Video production decisions](video-production-decisions.md).
