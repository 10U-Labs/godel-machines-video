**Why:** The user gave no reason. Claude's interpretation (not the user's words): they want the real mechanism of each machine explained in plain language, not replaced by a comparison to something else.

**How to apply:** In the narration script and on-screen text for this video, describe what each machine actually does, define terms plainly, and assume no maths or programming background. Do not write "it's like..." comparisons or metaphors that stand in for the mechanism. Visuals should depict the actual process (code, proofs, agents, benchmarks) rather than a metaphorical stand-in.
