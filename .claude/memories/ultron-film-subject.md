# The Ultron film's subject

This memory holds for the Ultron film alone, and what it sets is the user's direction. The film asks whether Ultron is a Gödel Machine. Its Ultron is the one in the 2015 film Avengers: Age of Ultron, not the one in the comics.

Of that film's plot, three steps matter, and they are simple. Ultron starts as an AI with no body. He turns himself into a feeble robot. Then he turns himself into a powerful one.

**Why:** No reason comes with the direction. Claude's interpretation: the three forms are the self-improvement that the title's question is about, and every other part of the plot pulls a general viewer away from it.

**How to apply:** Tell Ultron's story as those three forms, each one made by Ultron himself, and compare that with what a Gödel Machine does. Leave out the rest of the plot unless the user asks for it: the scepter and its gem, what Ultron wants and why, the twins, the vibranium, the body that becomes Vision, the copies of himself, the flying city, and how he ends. A line may name the film, the two heroes who try to make him, and the heroes who destroy his first body, since the three steps cannot be told without them.
