# The Ultron film's production decisions

These production choices are settled for the Ultron film, whose material is in `productions/ultron-self-improving-ai/`:

- Audience: the general public. The narration assumes no maths, no programming, and no memory of the Marvel film it is about.
- Length: no limit. The content decides how long the film runs.
- Look: the flat shapes of the shared kit, in a darker palette of the film's own: steel greys and reds on near-black. Ultron is a robot of Claude's own design, which copies none of Marvel's artwork, logos, or footage.
- Narration voice: Kokoro `af_heart` at speed 0.95, the narrator of the Gödel machines film, so that the two films sound like a pair. Kokoro is Apache 2.0, so the film owes no credit for the voice.
- Music: a new, darker tune in a minor key, written for this film in the `[music]` table of its `production.toml`.
- Ending: after the last word every light goes out, and after a moment of dark Ultron's red eyes open again, alone. The script ends with a pause mark, which gives the picture its seconds of silence.

**Why:** Each is a matter of taste that the user decides, and each is decided, so none is reopened without the user. The audience, the length, the palette, the voice, the music, and the ending are the user's choices. That Ultron is an original design is Claude's proposal, which every look put to the user carried.

**How to apply:** Treat these as decided for the Ultron film; don't ask again. Don't cut its script to reach a target length. The film's colours and its backdrop go in a script in its own folder, since `src/hyperframes/kit.js` holds one palette for every production, per [The repo is a media factory](the-repo-is-a-media-factory.md). Its scenes and its `parts.py` are written for Blender, which the factory does not render with; they are written again as page scripts once its script is marked ready, and not before, per [No video before the script is ready](no-video-before-the-script-is-ready.md). They are this film's choices alone, so ask about the look, voice, music, length, and audience of a new production. What the film is about is in [The Ultron film's subject](ultron-film-subject.md), and the choices of the first film are in [Video production decisions](video-production-decisions.md).
