# One idea at a time

Each scene of a production carries one idea. It is the user's rule, and it holds for every production (see [The repo is a media factory](the-repo-is-a-media-factory.md)).

**Why:** The user's reason is that a scene with more throws far too much at the viewer at once. Claude's interpretation: a scene that stacks several facts, numbers, caveats, and side stories (costs, run lengths, test details, who wrote what) leaves a general viewer unable to tell which one matters.

**How to apply:** Split any scene that carries two ideas into two scenes, and cut details that do not serve the idea of the scene, even when they are true and interesting. Keep numbers to the few that show a result. Hold the pictures to the same rule: one thing to look at, one thing happening, and the text on screen limited to what that one idea needs. Related: [Every production tells a story](every-production-tells-a-story.md), [Show people, not panels](show-people-not-panels.md).
