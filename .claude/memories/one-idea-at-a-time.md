# One idea at a time

Each scene carries one idea. On 2026-11-14, after watching the Blender test shot and having read script draft 5, the user said each scene threw far too much information at the viewer at once.

**Why:** Claude's interpretation: draft 5 scenes stacked several facts, numbers, caveats, and side stories (costs, run lengths, test details, who wrote what), so a general viewer could not tell which one mattered.

**How to apply:** Split any scene that carries two ideas into two scenes, and cut details that do not serve the idea of the scene, even when they are true and interesting. Keep numbers to the few that show a result. Hold the pictures to the same rule: one thing to look at, one thing happening, and the text on screen limited to what that one idea needs. Script draft 6 was written this way. Related: [The film tells a story](the-film-tells-a-story.md), [Show people, not panels](show-people-not-panels.md).
