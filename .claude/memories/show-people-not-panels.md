# Show people, not panels

On 2026-11-14 the user watched the first four rendered scenes (opening, self-editing, Good's idea, the hard problem, in script draft 5) and judged both the storytelling and the visuals very boring. They pointed out that the film is for people, so when the narration mentions people (researchers, Good, Turing, Schmidhuber), the pictures should show them. They also asked again for a free tool that can make 2D visuals like Kurzgesagt's.

**Why:** Claude's interpretation: those scenes were built from program windows, text panels, meters, and on-screen captions that repeat the narration. That reads as a slide deck, not an animated film, and it leaves out the people who carry the story.

**How to apply:** Draw illustrated scenes: people, places, depth, a moving camera, and constant small motion. Show the people the narration names, in the places where things happened. Use on-screen text sparingly and do not caption what the voice is already saying. Treat the scenes built so far as a draft to be redone in this style, not as a base to polish.
