# At most three things

A production never asks its viewer to keep more than three things in mind at once. It is the user's rule: people handle a few things at a time badly once there are more than three. It holds for every production (see [The repo is a media factory](the-repo-is-a-media-factory.md)).

**Why:** The user's reason is that people are poor at holding many things at once, and the limit is usually three.

**How to apply:** A list that the narration names, such as signs, behaviours, steps, or reasons, has at most three items. When the material offers more, keep the three that the story shows most strongly, and fold the rest into them or leave them unnamed. Grouping six things into three pairs still asks for six, so it does not meet the rule. The sentience film's three signs and the society film's three behaviours follow it; see [The sentience film's subject](sentience-film-subject.md) and [The society film's subject](society-film-subject.md).
