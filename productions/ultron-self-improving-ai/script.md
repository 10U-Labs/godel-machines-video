# Is Ultron a Self-Improving AI?

Narration script, draft 9. One paragraph is one voiced line. Lines starting
with ">" are notes and are not voiced.

The film takes Ultron, the artificial intelligence in the 2015 film Avengers:
Age of Ultron, and asks whether he is what computer science calls a
self-improving AI. The answer is yes, and the film gives the reasons. Of that
film's plot it tells three steps: Ultron started as a program with no body, he
made himself a feeble body, and then he made himself a better one. Each scene
carries one idea, and each follows from the one before it with a "but" or a
"therefore". The joins:

> 00 Ultron started as a program with no body, BUT later he was a feeble robot and then a powerful one, and no person built either. THEREFORE the question: is he a self-improving AI?
> 01 THEREFORE what the term means: an AI that changes itself, its program or its machine, and is better for the change.
> 02 THEREFORE the first reason. Ultron had no hands, BUT he could control the lab's machines, THEREFORE they built him a body and he moved into it: he was able to change himself.
> 03 BUT that body was feeble. THEREFORE he built a better one, and each change left him able to do more: he was able to improve himself.
> 04 BUT people make machines better all the time, and that does not make the machines self-improving. THEREFORE it counts that Ultron made the changes himself.
> 05 BUT his program never changed. THEREFORE the last check: the change can be to the machine, and his was. That kind has a name: physical self-improvement.
> 06 THEREFORE yes.
> 07 BUT nobody told him to improve himself. THEREFORE the paper's last point: an AI that is trying to do anything will try to improve itself without being told.

## 00-the-question | The question

An artificial intelligence starts up in an empty lab. It can think and talk but doesn't have a body.

This is Ultron, from the 2015 film Avengers: Age of Ultron.

Later in the film, Ultron becomes a feeble robot and then a powerful one. No person built those robots. Ultron himself did.

Computer scientists call an AI that makes itself better a self-improving AI.

So, is Ultron a self-improving AI?

## 01-what-it-means | What it means

Yes, he is. To see why, start with what the term means.

An AI is a program. And programs run on machines.

In 2007, a computer scientist named Stephen Omohundro wrote that an AI can change itself in two ways. It can change its own program. Or it can change the machine the program runs on.

Some changes leave an AI worse at what it is trying to do. Others leave it better. An AI that makes itself better is a self-improving one.

## 02-he-changes-himself | He changed himself

Now look at what Ultron did. He wanted a body, but had no hands to build one.

The machines in the lab, however, were run by computers, and Ultron is a program. So he took control of the computers, and they built him a body.

He moved into it. The same program now ran on a new machine: one that could walk.

So Ultron was able to change himself. That ability is the first reason he is a self-improving AI.

## 03-for-the-better | For the better

But that first body was feeble. So, eventually, Ultron built a better body for himself.

Each change left him able to do more. In his first form, he could not move. In his second, he could walk. In his third, he was strong enough to fight.

So Ultron was able to improve himself, and more than once. That ability to improve is the second reason.

## 04-by-himself | By himself

But people make machines better all the time, and that does not make those machines self-improving.

What counts is who makes the change. No person built either of Ultron's bodies. Ultron did.

That he built them himself is the third reason.

## 05-physical-self-improvement | Physical self-improvement

There is one thing Ultron never changed: his program. The film never shows it being rewritten. The same program moved from one body to the next.

That does not count against him. A self-improving AI can change its program, or it can change its machine. Ultron changed the machine.

Omohundro's paper has a name for that. It is called physical self-improvement.

## 06-the-answer | The answer

So, is Ultron a self-improving AI? Yes.

He changed himself. The changes left him able to do more. And he made them himself.

## 07-nobody-told-him | Nobody told him to

Omohundro wrote one more thing. Nobody has to tell an AI to improve itself.

Whatever an AI is trying to do, a better version of that AI would do it better. So it will try to become that version, unless it was built not to.

He wrote that years before the film was made.

And in the film, nobody told Ultron to build a body. He asked about one almost as soon as he started up.
